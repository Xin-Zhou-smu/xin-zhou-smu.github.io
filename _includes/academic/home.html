I am Xin Zhou, a Research Scientist (equivalent to a Postdoctoral Fellow) in Software Engineering at Singapore Management University, working with Prof. David Lo.

My research focuses on understanding, evaluating, and improving AI systems for software engineering, particularly coding agents, their reliability and generalization, and their applications to software security and maintenance.

I received my Ph.D. from SMU in January 2025, where I was a member of SOAR. Before that, I completed my bachelor’s degree at Beijing Institute of Technology.
